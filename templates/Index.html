Plant Diseases

Plant diseases are among the most serious challenges facing global agriculture.
Jordan’s farmers face many of the same plant health threats as the rest of the world, plus some unique regional challenges:

  1. Nematode Damage: Citrus trees, broad beans, bananas, and cabbages in Jordan have been affected by root-knot and cyst nematodes.
  2. Cereal Rusts and Mildews: Wheat and barley face fungal diseases that can devastate harvests if not managed.
  3. Tomato Brown Rugose Fruit Virus (ToBRFV): First identified in Jordan, this new virus causes rough, brown fruits, threatening exports.
  4. Tomato Viruses: Studies show that nearly 40% of open-field tomatoes in Jordan carry one or more viruses, such as Tomato spotted wilt virus and Potato virus Y.
